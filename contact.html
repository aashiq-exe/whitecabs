Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about our taxi booking rates, driver allowances, and travel terms.

How do I book a cab with White Cabs Manipal? +
You can book by clicking on any of our call buttons to call us directly at +91 9448012378, or chat with us on WhatsApp. Share your pick-up point, destination, date, and preferred vehicle class (Sedan, SUV, or Van).
Are tolls, parking fees, and state entry permits included in the fare? +
Unless specified in a custom package rate, toll gates, airport parking fees, and state border permits are paid by the customer directly during transit. This keeps per-km pricing highly transparent.
Is driver allowance charged separately for outstation trips? +
Yes, for outstation/multi-day packages, a standard driver beta (driver allowance) is charged per day to cover driver lodging and meals. This is always communicated upfront.
Can I cancel or change my booking reservation? +
Absolutely. We believe in maximum flexibility. You can cancel or reschedule your cab booking free of charge up to 12 hours before the scheduled pick-up time.
