Our Commitment
To Travel Safety

At White Cabs Manipal, traveling safely is at the core of our business values. Every vehicle undergoes a rigorous inspection protocol before leaving the garage to ensure brakes, tires, and internal functions are in pristine condition.

  • Vetted Chauffeurs: All drivers are licensed, background-checked, and highly familiar with local and outstation terrain.
  • Real-time GPS Tracking: We monitor every route live to ensure driver compliance with safety limits and standard paths.
  • Sanitized Fleet: Cars are thoroughly cleaned, disinfected, and odor-free for each booking.
  • 24x7 Helpline Support: Dedicated operator available to resolve routes, roadside support, or emergency queries.
